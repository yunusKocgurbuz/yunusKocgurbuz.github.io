Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our game. This policy applies to our mobile games generally, so the same link can be used across the games we publish. Some features described below apply only when a given game includes them.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our game, we may process the following information:

  • Anonymous authentication identifiers used to operate cloud features.
  • A device identifier, or a hashed form of it, used to restore purchases or other game settings on the same device.
  • Purchase status for in-app products, such as an ads-removal entitlement, including platform and a timestamp.
  • Game progress and settings stored on your device.
  • Device notification identifiers or local notification permissions, if you allow notifications.
  • Advertising identifiers and related device information processed by advertising partners where ads are shown.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the game.

We do not require you to create a named player profile, and we do not collect your name, email address, or payment card details ourselves.

2. Local Game Data

Progress, settings, and similar game data are typically stored on your device. Uninstalling the game, clearing app data, or changing devices may remove this information unless a feature described in this policy restores it.

3. In-App Purchases

Our game may offer in-app purchases, such as removing ads. Payments are processed by Apple or Google through their app stores. We do not receive your full payment card number.

To keep a purchase available after reinstalling the game, we may store a purchase entitlement on your device and, where enabled, in a cloud record associated with an anonymous device identifier.

4. Cloud Features and Firebase

Where enabled, our game uses Google Firebase for features such as anonymous authentication and cloud data storage. This may include a hashed device identifier, platform, and purchase-related flags used to restore entitlements.

These service providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

5. Advertising

Our game may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, and other data in accordance with their own privacy policies and your applicable privacy choices.

Where required, you may be shown a consent or privacy-options form before personalized ads are requested. You can also control advertising and tracking permissions through your device settings.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, our game may send local or push reminders related to the game. You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

7. How We Use Information

We use information collected through our game to:

  • Provide and maintain the game.
  • Save and restore progress, settings, and purchase entitlements.
  • Process and restore in-app purchases.
  • Deliver notifications if you allow them.
  • Operate advertising where applicable.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate our game, such as Firebase, Google AdMob, Apple, Google Play, and related hosting or store infrastructure.

9. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the game's features, restore purchases, comply with applicable requirements, and protect the security and integrity of the service. Local game data remains on your device until you delete the game or clear its data.

10. Deletion Requests

Our game does not use a named user account. Local game data can be removed by uninstalling the game or clearing the app's data in your device settings.

If you want us to delete cloud records associated with your device, such as a stored purchase-restore flag, contact us at the email address below and include the game name and platform (Android or iOS). We will review the request and delete associated data we control, except where we must retain limited information to meet legal, security, or store requirements.

11. Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed at children and are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.
