1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to Lost Titu: Puzzle Platformer ("Lost Titu", "the Game"), developed and published by Avesta Soft.

Lost Titu is a mobile puzzle-platform adventure game. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when players use the Game, including through integrated third-party services.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features and services enabled in Lost Titu, the following information may be processed:

  • Anonymous identifiers: Identifiers used for optional cloud-based features.
  • Device information: Device identifiers or hashed identifiers used for application functionality and purchase restoration.
  • Purchase information: Product identifiers, purchase status, transaction timestamps, and entitlements.
  • Game data: Progress, completed levels, preferences, and settings.
  • Notification information: Notification permissions and push notification tokens, where applicable.
  • Advertising information: Advertising identifiers, device information, and advertising interactions processed by advertising services.
  • Technical data: Operating system information, diagnostics, performance information, and technical data.
  • App interaction data: Game launches and other application events used for analytics or advertising measurement, where enabled.

Lost Titu does not require players to provide their real names, email addresses, or payment card details to play the Game.

3. Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, and preferences may be stored locally on the player's device.

Uninstalling the Game, clearing application data, or changing devices may result in the loss of locally stored information unless a supported restoration feature is available.

4. In-App Purchases

Lost Titu may offer optional in-app purchases, including features such as advertisement removal.

Payments are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play. Avesta Soft does not directly receive or store full payment card information.

Purchase-related information may be stored locally or through supported cloud services to verify and restore purchased entitlements.

5. Firebase and Cloud Services

Where enabled, Lost Titu may use Google Firebase services to support:

  • Anonymous authentication.
  • Cloud-based storage.
  • Purchase entitlement restoration.
  • Application functionality and technical support.

Firebase may process device identifiers, technical information, and other data according to the services configured in the Game.

More information: Google Firebase Privacy

Google and its advertising partners may process information including:

Where legally required, consent or privacy options may be provided before certain advertising technologies are used.

This information may be used for advertising measurement, attribution, analytics, and other advertising-related purposes supported by the configured Meta services.
