Privacy Policy for Snako

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snako ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Snako mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Snako, we may process the following information:

  • Account identifiers associated with Anonymous, Apple, or Google sign-in.
  • Your Snako username and player profile information.
  • Game information such as scores, high scores, leaderboard data, and multiplayer match-related information.
  • Friend relationships, friend requests, and game invitations.
  • Device notification identifiers used to deliver push notifications.
  • Gameplay screenshots associated with high-score features where applicable.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the app.

2. Sign-In

Snako supports anonymous access and may also support Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. Authentication is provided using Firebase Authentication.

Information received through a sign-in provider is used to authenticate your account and provide Snako's account features. Your public Snako username is managed separately from your Apple or Google account name.

3. Game and Social Features

Snako may store information necessary to provide game features, including player usernames, scores, leaderboard information, friendships, friend requests, game invitations, and multiplayer-related information.

This information is used to operate Snako's gameplay, social, leaderboard, and multiplayer features.

4. Push Notifications

If you allow notifications, Snako may use a device notification token to send notifications such as friend requests, game invitations, and other game-related updates.

You can control notification permissions through your device settings.

5. Screenshots and Game Content

Snako may store gameplay screenshots associated with certain game features, such as high-score records. These screenshots are used to provide the corresponding in-app feature.

6. Advertising

Snako may display advertisements using Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device information and other data in accordance with their own privacy policies and your applicable privacy choices.

7. Firebase and Other Service Providers

Snako uses services provided by Google Firebase for features such as authentication, cloud data storage, notifications, and related backend functionality.

These service providers may process information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

8. Multiplayer

When you use multiplayer features, technical information necessary to establish and operate a game session may be processed by Snako's game servers. Multiplayer credentials are used to authorize game sessions and operate matches.

9. How We Use Information

We use information collected through Snako to:

  • Provide and maintain the game.
  • Authenticate players.
  • Maintain player profiles and leaderboards.
  • Provide friends, invitations, and multiplayer functionality.
  • Deliver notifications.
  • Operate advertising where applicable.
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve reliability.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate Snako, such as Firebase, Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, Google AdMob, hosting, and game-server infrastructure.

11. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's features, maintain accounts and game records, comply with applicable requirements, and protect the security and integrity of the service.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your Snako account directly from within the app. To delete your account and associated Snako account data, open the Profile section in Snako and select Delete Profile.

Please review the confirmation shown in the app before completing the deletion. Account deletion is intended to remove your Snako account and associated data according to the deletion process implemented in the app.

13. Children's Privacy

Snako is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information improperly, please contact us.

14. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion of certain information.

You can also delete your Snako account directly from the app by going to Profile → Delete Profile.

You may contact us using the email address below regarding privacy or data-related requests.

15. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information associated with Snako. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and the "Last updated" date will be revised when appropriate.
